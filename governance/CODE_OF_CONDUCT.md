# B2C Solution — Code of Conduct

**Effective Date:** 22 August 2026  
**Last Updated:** 22 August 2026  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Organization:** B2C Solution

---

## 1. Purpose

B2C Solution is committed to maintaining a professional, respectful, secure, and productive environment for everyone interacting with the organization.

This Code of Conduct establishes expected standards of behavior for:

- Employees;
- Contractors;
- Developers;
- Designers;
- Contributors;
- Partners;
- Clients;
- Community participants;
- Other individuals representing or interacting with B2C Solution.

---

## 2. Expected Behavior

Everyone interacting with B2C Solution is expected to:

- Communicate professionally;
- Treat others with respect;
- Provide constructive feedback;
- Respect reasonable boundaries;
- Protect confidential information;
- Follow applicable laws and agreements;
- Respect intellectual-property rights;
- Follow security requirements;
- Act honestly and in good faith;
- Take responsibility for their actions.

---

## 3. Professional Communication

Communication should be:

- Clear;
- Relevant;
- Constructive;
- Respectful;
- Appropriate to the professional context.

Disagreements are acceptable and can contribute to better decisions when handled professionally.

Personal attacks and hostile communication are not acceptable.

---

## 4. Unacceptable Behavior

B2C Solution does not tolerate:

- Harassment;
- Threats;
- Intimidation;
- Discrimination;
- Abusive behavior;
- Deliberate disruption;
- Malicious conduct;
- Unauthorized access;
- Credential theft;
- Fraud;
- Deliberate security abuse;
- Intellectual-property theft;
- Disclosure of confidential information;
- Retaliation against good-faith reporters.

---

## 5. Security Responsibilities

Individuals interacting with B2C Solution must not:

- Share confidential credentials;
- Commit secrets to public repositories;
- Attempt unauthorized system access;
- Intentionally introduce malicious code;
- Exploit vulnerabilities for personal gain;
- Access information without authorization;
- Circumvent security controls without permission.

Security concerns should be reported through the appropriate security-disclosure process.

---

## 6. Intellectual Property

Participants must respect the intellectual property of:

- B2C Solution;
- Clients;
- Contributors;
- Third-party developers;
- Software providers;
- Other rights holders.

Unauthorized copying, redistribution, or use of protected materials may violate applicable law or contractual requirements.

---

## 7. Confidentiality

Confidential information must be handled appropriately.

Examples include:

- Client information;
- Credentials;
- Unreleased products;
- Private source code;
- Business strategies;
- Financial information;
- Internal documentation;
- Personal information.

Confidential information should only be accessed and shared when authorized.

---

## 8. Conflicts of Interest

Individuals should disclose relevant conflicts of interest where they may affect business decisions or project responsibilities.

Examples may include:

- Financial interests;
- Competing business relationships;
- Undisclosed personal benefits;
- Relationships that materially affect decision-making.

B2C Solution may establish additional conflict-of-interest procedures as the organization grows.

---

## 9. Contributions

Contributions to B2C Solution projects should:

- Follow applicable project guidelines;
- Respect licenses;
- Avoid malicious code;
- Avoid unauthorized third-party material;
- Follow security practices;
- Provide accurate information;
- Respect review and approval processes.

Contributors should not intentionally introduce vulnerabilities or licensing problems.

---

## 10. Client Relationships

B2C Solution expects professional conduct in Client relationships.

This includes:

- Clear communication;
- Accurate commitments;
- Respect for agreed scope;
- Proper handling of Client information;
- Timely communication regarding significant issues;
- Honest representation of capabilities and limitations.

Clients are also expected to communicate and interact professionally with B2C Solution personnel.

---

## 11. Reporting Concerns

Concerns about violations may be reported through an appropriate B2C Solution communication channel.

Depending on the nature of the issue:

- General conduct concerns may be reported to B2C Solution management;
- Security concerns should follow the Responsible Disclosure Policy;
- Privacy concerns should follow the Privacy Policy;
- Contractual disputes should follow the applicable agreement.

Reports should be made in good faith.

---

## 12. Investigation

B2C Solution may review credible reports of misconduct.

An investigation may involve:

- Reviewing relevant information;
- Speaking with involved individuals;
- Reviewing project records;
- Examining applicable agreements;
- Taking reasonable corrective action.

Investigations should be handled as fairly and confidentially as reasonably possible.

---

## 13. Consequences of Violations

Depending on the circumstances, violations may result in:

- Warning;
- Removal of content;
- Restriction of access;
- Removal from a project;
- Termination of contributor access;
- Contractual action;
- Termination of a business relationship;
- Other appropriate action.

Serious unlawful conduct may also be referred to appropriate authorities where required or appropriate.

---

## 14. Non-Retaliation

B2C Solution does not support retaliation against individuals who make good-faith reports of:

- Security vulnerabilities;
- Privacy concerns;
- Misconduct;
- Policy violations;
- Other legitimate concerns.

False reports made intentionally to harm another person may themselves constitute misconduct.

---

## 15. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to interactions occurring through:

- B2C Solution projects;
- GitHub repositories;
- Official websites;
- Business communications;
- Client interactions;
- Professional events;
- Other channels where individuals are acting on behalf of or directly interacting with B2C Solution.

---

## 16. Updates

B2C Solution may update this Code of Conduct as the organization grows and its operational requirements change.

The latest version will contain the applicable effective date and last-updated date.
