# B2C Solution — Acceptable Use Policy

**Effective Date:** 22 August 2026  
**Last Updated:** 22 August 2026  
**Version:** 1.0

## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines prohibited and restricted activities when using services, websites, applications, software, infrastructure, or other digital products provided or operated by **B2C Solution**.

The purpose of this policy is to protect:

- Users;
- Clients;
- B2C Solution;
- Third-party service providers;
- Systems and infrastructure;
- Data and intellectual property.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy may apply to:

- Customers;
- Clients;
- Website users;
- Application users;
- Contributors;
- Partners;
- Contractors;
- Other individuals or organizations using B2C Solution services.

Project-specific agreements may contain additional requirements.

---

## 3. Permitted Use

Users may use B2C Solution services only for:

- Lawful purposes;
- Authorized business activities;
- Legitimate personal activities where permitted;
- Purposes described in the applicable agreement;
- Activities that do not violate the rights of others.

---

## 4. Prohibited Activities

Users must not use B2C Solution services to:

- Conduct unlawful activities;
- Commit fraud;
- Facilitate theft;
- Distribute malware;
- Conduct unauthorized attacks;
- Gain unauthorized access to systems;
- Circumvent security controls;
- Abuse authentication systems;
- Harvest credentials;
- Distribute stolen information;
- Infringe intellectual-property rights;
- Violate privacy rights;
- Harass or threaten others;
- Distribute malicious content;
- Interfere with service availability.

---

## 5. Unauthorized Access

Users must not attempt to access:

- Another user's account;
- Restricted administrative interfaces;
- Private databases;
- Internal systems;
- Confidential files;
- Private APIs;
- Infrastructure without authorization.

Authorization must be obtained before accessing systems that are not intended for the user.

---

## 6. Security Testing

Security testing is only permitted when explicitly authorized.

Users must not perform:

- Destructive testing;
- Denial-of-service attacks;
- Credential attacks;
- Unauthorized vulnerability exploitation;
- Data destruction;
- Persistence attempts;
- Security-control bypasses.

For responsible vulnerability reporting, see:

`security/RESPONSIBLE_DISCLOSURE.md`

---

## 7. Malware and Malicious Code

Users must not upload, distribute, execute, or intentionally deploy malicious software through B2C Solution services.

Examples include:

- Viruses;
- Worms;
- Trojans;
- Ransomware;
- Spyware;
- Credential-stealing software;
- Destructive scripts;
- Other intentionally malicious software.

---

## 8. Spam and Abuse

B2C Solution services must not be used to distribute:

- Unsolicited bulk messages;
- Spam;
- Malicious links;
- Fraudulent communications;
- Phishing campaigns;
- Deceptive automated messages.

Users are responsible for complying with applicable communication and marketing laws.

---

## 9. Intellectual Property

Users must respect applicable intellectual-property rights.

Users must not knowingly use B2C Solution services to:

- Distribute pirated material;
- Upload stolen software;
- Circumvent licensing restrictions;
- Infringe copyrights;
- Misuse trademarks;
- Distribute unauthorized proprietary material.

---

## 10. Privacy and Personal Information

Users must not use B2C Solution services to unlawfully collect, expose, sell, or misuse personal information.

Examples include unauthorized:

- Data harvesting;
- Credential collection;
- Personal-data disclosure;
- Tracking;
- Database extraction;
- Identity-related abuse.

Users are responsible for complying with applicable privacy laws when processing personal information.

---

## 11. Fraud and Deception

Users must not use B2C Solution services for:

- Identity fraud;
- Payment fraud;
- Financial deception;
- Fake business operations;
- Phishing;
- Impersonation;
- Forged documents;
- Misrepresentation;
- Other deceptive activities.

---

## 12. Resource Abuse

Users must not intentionally consume excessive resources in a manner that:

- Disrupts other users;
- Degrades service performance;
- Causes unnecessary infrastructure costs;
- Circumvents service limits;
- Interferes with normal operation.

Reasonable usage limits may be established for individual services.

---

## 13. Automated Access

Automated tools, bots, crawlers, scripts, or APIs may only be used where permitted.

Users must not use automation to:

- Circumvent rate limits;
- Overload systems;
- Harvest restricted information;
- Bypass authentication;
- Abuse APIs;
- Interfere with normal service operation.

---

## 14. Account Sharing

Users should not share account credentials unless explicitly permitted by the applicable service.

Users are responsible for protecting their authentication credentials.

If unauthorized access is suspected, the user should notify B2C Solution promptly.

---

## 15. Content Requirements

Where B2C Solution permits users to submit or publish content, users are responsible for ensuring that their content:

- Is lawful;
- Does not knowingly infringe third-party rights;
- Does not contain malicious software;
- Does not intentionally facilitate fraud;
- Does not expose confidential information without authorization.

Additional content rules may apply to specific services.

---

## 16. Third-Party Services

B2C Solution may integrate with third-party services.

Users must comply with the applicable terms and policies of those services.

B2C Solution is not responsible for a user's unauthorized use of third-party platforms.

---

## 17. Enforcement

If B2C Solution reasonably believes that this policy has been violated, it may take appropriate action, including:

- Warning the user;
- Restricting access;
- Suspending an account;
- Removing content;
- Terminating services;
- Investigating the incident;
- Preserving relevant records;
- Taking contractual or legal action where appropriate.

Actions will depend on the seriousness and circumstances of the violation.

---

## 18. Emergency Security Situations

B2C Solution may take immediate protective action when necessary to protect:

- Users;
- Client information;
- Infrastructure;
- Business operations;
- Third-party systems;
- Security of the service.

Immediate action may be taken before completing a full investigation where there is a significant security risk.
