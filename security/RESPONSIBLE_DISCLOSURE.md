# B2C Solution — Responsible Disclosure Policy

**Effective Date:** 22 August 2026  
**Last Updated:** 22 August 2026  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Organization:** B2C Solution

---

## 1. Purpose

B2C Solution values responsible security research and encourages individuals to report genuine security vulnerabilities that may affect B2C Solution websites, applications, services, or infrastructure.

This policy provides a structured process for reporting potential security issues so that they can be investigated and addressed responsibly.

---

## 2. Reporting Contact

Security vulnerabilities should be reported privately to:

**Email:** b2csolution2436@gmail.com

**Website:** https://b2csolutionseller.lovable.app

Please do not publicly disclose a suspected vulnerability before B2C Solution has had a reasonable opportunity to investigate and address it.

---

## 3. What to Report

Examples of issues that may be appropriate to report include:

- Authentication vulnerabilities;
- Authorization bypasses;
- Unauthorized access to protected information;
- Significant data-exposure vulnerabilities;
- Remote code execution;
- SQL injection;
- Cross-site scripting;
- Server-side request forgery;
- Security misconfiguration;
- Significant API security vulnerabilities;
- Authentication-token exposure;
- Other vulnerabilities that could materially affect security.

---

## 4. Responsible Testing

Security researchers should limit testing to what is reasonably necessary to demonstrate the vulnerability.

Researchers should:

- Avoid accessing unrelated user data;
- Avoid modifying or deleting data;
- Avoid disrupting services;
- Avoid degrading system availability;
- Avoid obtaining credentials;
- Avoid social engineering;
- Avoid physical attacks;
- Stop testing once sufficient evidence has been obtained.

---

## 5. Prohibited Testing

The following activities are not considered responsible security research:

- Denial-of-service attacks;
- Distributed denial-of-service attacks;
- Destructive testing;
- Data destruction;
- Extortion;
- Ransom demands;
- Spam;
- Phishing;
- Social engineering of employees or users;
- Credential theft;
- Malware deployment;
- Attacks against third-party systems;
- Persistent unauthorized access;
- Actions intended to disrupt business operations.

---

## 6. Third-Party Systems

A vulnerability involving a third-party service should generally be reported to the relevant service provider.

Researchers should not attack or test third-party infrastructure merely because it is connected to a B2C Solution service.

Where a B2C Solution integration appears to contribute to the issue, the report may also be sent to B2C Solution.

---

## 7. Information to Include

A useful vulnerability report should contain:

- Clear vulnerability title;
- Affected website, application, or component;
- Description of the issue;
- Steps required to reproduce the issue;
- Expected behavior;
- Actual behavior;
- Security impact;
- Relevant screenshots or logs where appropriate;
- Proof of concept where reasonably necessary;
- Suggested remediation, if known.

Do not include unnecessary personal or confidential information.

---

## 8. Safe Testing Environment

Where B2C Solution provides a dedicated testing environment, researchers should use that environment instead of production systems whenever possible.

Testing against production systems should remain limited and non-destructive.

---

## 9. Vulnerability Severity

B2C Solution may assess reported vulnerabilities using factors such as:

- Exploitability;
- Required privileges;
- User interaction;
- Confidentiality impact;
- Integrity impact;
- Availability impact;
- Number of affected users;
- Business impact.

Severity classifications may include:

| Severity | General Meaning |
|---|---|
| Critical | Severe compromise or significant systemic impact |
| High | Significant security impact |
| Medium | Moderate security impact |
| Low | Limited security impact |
| Informational | Security observation without material impact |

These classifications are indicative and do not guarantee a particular response or reward.

---

## 10. Response Process

B2C Solution may follow a process similar to:

```text
Security Report
      ↓
Initial Review
      ↓
Reproduction
      ↓
Severity Assessment
      ↓
Remediation
      ↓
Validation
      ↓
Closure
```

The actual process may vary depending on the severity and complexity of the issue.

---

## 11. Communication

B2C Solution will attempt to communicate with the reporter where sufficient contact information is provided.

The investigation may require additional information to reproduce or understand the issue.

Researchers should avoid repeatedly contacting multiple B2C Solution channels about the same vulnerability unless requested.

---

## 12. Coordinated Disclosure

B2C Solution encourages coordinated disclosure.

Researchers should provide reasonable time for:

- Investigation;
- Development of a fix;
- Security testing;
- Deployment;
- User notification where required.

Public disclosure should preferably occur after coordination with B2C Solution.

---

## 13. Public Disclosure

Researchers should not publicly disclose:

- Exploitation details;
- Credentials;
- Personal information;
- Private technical information;
- Unpatched vulnerabilities;
- Confidential B2C Solution information;

without appropriate authorization or coordination.

B2C Solution may coordinate a public security advisory where appropriate.

---

## 14. Good-Faith Security Research

B2C Solution appreciates good-faith security research that:

- Identifies genuine vulnerabilities;
- Minimizes harm;
- Protects user information;
- Avoids unnecessary disruption;
- Provides useful technical information;
- Allows reasonable remediation time.

---

## 15. No Guarantee of Reward

Unless B2C Solution explicitly announces a bug-bounty or reward program, submitting a vulnerability report does not create an entitlement to:

- Payment;
- Bounty;
- Employment;
- Public recognition;
- Compensation.

B2C Solution may choose to recognize helpful researchers at its discretion.

---

## 16. Legal Considerations

This policy does not authorize activity that violates applicable law.

Researchers are responsible for ensuring that their actions comply with applicable legal requirements.

B2C Solution may take appropriate action where activity is malicious, destructive, unlawful, or outside the scope of responsible disclosure.

---

## 17. Changes to This Policy

B2C Solution may update this Responsible Disclosure Policy as:

- Security practices evolve;
- Infrastructure changes;
- New services are introduced;
- Legal requirements change;
- Security risks develop.

The latest version will contain the applicable effective date and last-updated date.
