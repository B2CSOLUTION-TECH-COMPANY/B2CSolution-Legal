# B2C Solution — Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Effective Date:** 22 August 2026  
**Last Updated:** 22 August 2026  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Organization:** B2C Solution

> **Important:** This is a general business-policy framework and is not legal advice. The final policy should be reviewed and adapted by an appropriate legal professional based on B2C Solution's actual business structure, services, payment methods, and applicable laws.

---

## 1. Purpose

This Refund and Cancellation Policy explains the general approach of **B2C Solution** regarding project cancellations, service cancellations, refunds, and payments.

This policy should be read together with the applicable:

- Terms and Conditions
- Client Service Agreement
- Statement of Work
- Quotation
- Invoice
- Project-specific agreement

Where a signed agreement contains specific refund or cancellation terms, those terms may take precedence.

---

## 2. Project-Based Services

B2C Solution may provide customized digital services, including:

- Website development
- Web application development
- Software development
- UI/UX design
- Technical services
- Website maintenance
- Digital solutions
- Other agreed technology services

Because customized services may involve time, resources, third-party costs, and work performed specifically for a Client, refunds may depend on the stage and circumstances of the project.

---

## 3. Advance Payments

An advance payment may be required before project work begins.

An advance may be used to cover:

- Project planning
- Design work
- Development resources
- Reserved project capacity
- Third-party services
- Initial setup
- Other project-related costs

The applicable quotation or agreement will specify the required payment structure.

---

## 4. Cancellation Before Work Begins

If a Client requests cancellation before substantive project work begins, B2C Solution may consider a refund depending on:

- The applicable agreement;
- Whether resources have already been allocated;
- Third-party costs already incurred;
- Administrative or setup work performed;
- Any other non-refundable costs.

Any applicable refund will be determined according to the agreed project terms and applicable law.

---

## 5. Cancellation After Work Has Started

If a Client cancels a project after work has started, the refund, if any, may be adjusted to account for work already performed.

Factors may include:

- Design work completed;
- Development work completed;
- Project planning;
- Testing;
- Third-party expenses;
- Infrastructure costs;
- Hours or resources already committed;
- Deliverables already provided.

B2C Solution may be entitled to retain payment corresponding to work legitimately completed and costs reasonably incurred, subject to the applicable agreement and law.

---

## 6. Cancellation by B2C Solution

B2C Solution may suspend or terminate services where permitted by the applicable agreement, including circumstances such as:

- Non-payment;
- Material breach of contract;
- Abusive behavior;
- Unlawful activity;
- Security concerns;
- Misuse of services;
- Failure to provide required project information;
- Circumstances outside reasonable control.

Any resulting refund or payment obligations will be determined according to the applicable agreement and law.

---

## 7. Client Delays

Project timelines may be affected when the Client does not provide required:

- Content;
- Approvals;
- Feedback;
- Credentials;
- Technical information;
- Assets;
- Payments;
- Other required materials.

Client-caused delays do not automatically create a right to a refund.

The project timeline may be revised where appropriate.

---

## 8. Change of Project Requirements

A Client may request changes to the agreed project scope.

Changes may result in:

- Additional charges;
- Additional development time;
- Revised delivery dates;
- New requirements;
- A revised Statement of Work.

A change request should be approved before substantial additional work begins.

---

## 9. Non-Refundable Third-Party Costs

Certain third-party costs may be non-refundable.

Examples may include:

- Domain registration;
- Hosting;
- Cloud services;
- Premium software;
- Third-party APIs;
- Paid plugins;
- Licenses;
- Payment-provider charges;
- Other external services.

Where such costs have already been incurred on behalf of a Client, they may not be refundable.

---

## 10. Completed Deliverables

Once a deliverable has been completed, approved, accepted, or put into production, the Client may not automatically be entitled to a refund for that deliverable.

Specific acceptance and payment terms should be defined in the applicable project agreement.

---

## 11. Defective or Non-Conforming Services

If a delivered service materially fails to meet the agreed specifications, the Client should first notify B2C Solution and provide reasonable details of the issue.

Where appropriate, B2C Solution may:

1. Investigate the issue;
2. Correct an eligible defect;
3. Provide a reasonable workaround;
4. Re-perform the affected service where appropriate;
5. Apply another remedy specified in the applicable agreement.

A request for a refund should not replace the agreed defect-resolution process unless otherwise required by law or contract.

---

## 12. Bugs and Technical Issues

A technical issue does not automatically constitute grounds for a refund.

Where the issue falls within the agreed project scope and warranty/support period, B2C Solution may investigate and correct the issue according to the applicable agreement.

Issues caused by unauthorized modifications, third-party systems, Client changes, unsupported environments, or circumstances outside the agreed scope may be treated separately.

---

## 13. Maintenance and Subscription Services

For recurring services, cancellation terms may depend on the specific subscription or maintenance agreement.

The applicable agreement may specify:

- Billing frequency;
- Notice period;
- Renewal terms;
- Cancellation date;
- Outstanding amounts;
- Refund conditions.

---

## 14. Refund Request Process

A Client requesting a refund should contact B2C Solution through the official business communication channel.

The request should include:

- Client name;
- Project or invoice reference;
- Date of payment;
- Reason for the request;
- Relevant supporting information.

B2C Solution may request additional information where reasonably necessary to evaluate the request.

---

## 15. Refund Review

Refund requests will be reviewed based on:

- The applicable contract;
- Project status;
- Work already completed;
- Payment records;
- Third-party costs;
- Cancellation circumstances;
- Applicable law.

Submitting a refund request does not guarantee that a refund will be approved.

---

## 16. Refund Processing

Where a refund is approved, B2C Solution will generally attempt to process it through the original payment method where reasonably possible.

The time required for the funds to appear may depend on:

- Payment provider;
- Banking institution;
- Payment method;
- Processing procedures.

Third-party processing delays may be outside B2C Solution's control.

---

## 17. Fraudulent or Unauthorized Transactions

B2C Solution may investigate transactions suspected of being:

- Fraudulent;
- Unauthorized;
- Abusive;
- Made using stolen payment information;
- Otherwise unlawful.

Appropriate action may be taken in accordance with applicable law and payment-provider requirements.

---

## 18. Chargebacks

Clients are encouraged to contact B2C Solution before initiating a payment dispute where a billing or service issue can reasonably be resolved directly.

B2C Solution reserves the right to provide relevant contractual, payment, and service records to a payment provider or financial institution when responding to a legitimate chargeback or payment dispute.

Nothing in this section limits rights that cannot legally be restricted.

---

## 19. Exceptions Required by Law

Nothing in this policy is intended to remove or restrict any refund, cancellation, consumer, or other legal right that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited.

Where applicable law provides mandatory rights, those rights will apply.
