- Technical architecture;
- Database structures;
- API information;
- Credentials;
- Security information;
- Client information;

- Pricing;

- Marketing strategies;

- Internal documentation;
- Contracts;
- Commercial proposals;
- Trade secrets;
- Unreleased products;
- Other non-public business information.

# 4. Information Not Considered Confidential

Information will generally not be considered Confidential Information if the receiving Party can demonstrate that the information:

1. Was publicly available before disclosure;
2. Becomes publicly available without breach of this Agreement;
3. Was already lawfully known by the receiving Party;
4. Was independently developed without using the Confidential Information;
5. Was lawfully received from a third party without confidentiality restrictions.

---

The receiving Party agrees to:

- Protect Confidential Information;
- Use it only for the agreed purpose;
- Limit access to authorized individuals;
- Take reasonable security precautions;
- Avoid unauthorized disclosure;
- Not use Confidential Information for personal or competitive purposes outside the agreed relationship.
