# B2C Solution — Third-Party Licenses

**Effective Date:** 22 August 2026  
**Last Updated:** 22 August 2026  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Organization:** B2C Solution

---

## 1. Purpose

B2C Solution may use third-party software, libraries, frameworks, APIs, fonts, icons, media, services, and other resources in its websites, applications, and digital solutions.

This document establishes the general approach for identifying, documenting, and respecting the licenses and usage conditions associated with those resources.

---

## 2. General Principle

Third-party materials remain subject to the intellectual-property rights and license terms of their respective owners.

Using a third-party component does not automatically transfer ownership of that component to B2C Solution or its Clients.

B2C Solution aims to use third-party resources in accordance with their applicable licenses and terms.

---

## 3. Types of Third-Party Resources

Depending on the project, B2C Solution may use:

- Open-source software;
- Frameworks;
- Libraries;
- Package-manager dependencies;
- APIs;
- Cloud services;
- Fonts;
- Icons;
- Images;
- Videos;
- Design resources;
- SaaS platforms;
- Development tools;
- Other licensed digital assets.

---

## 4. Open-Source Software

B2C Solution may use open-source software where appropriate.

Examples of common open-source license families include:

- MIT License;
- Apache License 2.0;
- BSD licenses;
- GNU General Public License;
- GNU Lesser General Public License;
- Mozilla Public License;
- Other recognized open-source licenses.

Each dependency must be evaluated according to its actual license rather than assumed to have identical terms.

---

## 5. License Compliance

Where applicable, B2C Solution may maintain records containing:

- Package name;
- Version;
- License;
- Copyright holder;
- Source repository;
- Required attribution;
- Modification status;
- Applicable project;
- Compliance notes.

License obligations should be reviewed when dependencies are added, upgraded, replaced, or removed.

---

## 6. Copyright and Attribution

Where a third-party license requires copyright notices or attribution, B2C Solution will make reasonable efforts to preserve the required notices.

Required attribution may be provided through:

- License files;
- `NOTICE` files;
- Application documentation;
- Website credits;
- Repository documentation;
- Other appropriate locations.

The exact method depends on the applicable license.

---

## 7. Client Projects

When third-party resources are incorporated into a Client project, the Client may remain subject to the applicable third-party license terms.

Client agreements should make clear that B2C Solution cannot transfer ownership of third-party materials that it does not own.

Where relevant, the Client may be provided with appropriate license information during project handover.

---

## 8. Package Dependencies

For software projects, dependencies may be managed through package-management systems such as:

- npm;
- pnpm;
- Yarn;
- pip;
- Composer;
- Maven;
- Gradle;
- Other appropriate package managers.

Project repositories should maintain appropriate dependency manifests and lock files where applicable.

---

## 9. Dependency Security

Third-party dependencies may introduce security risks.

B2C Solution should periodically review dependencies for:

- Known vulnerabilities;
- Unsupported versions;
- Abandoned projects;
- License changes;
- Malicious packages;
- Significant security advisories.

Where appropriate, dependencies should be updated, replaced, or removed.

---

## 10. Fonts, Icons, Images, and Media

Visual assets may have specific license restrictions.

Before using third-party visual resources, B2C Solution should verify:

- Commercial-use permission;
- Attribution requirements;
- Modification rights;
- Redistribution restrictions;
- Client-use rights;
- License expiration or limitations, where applicable.

Assets should not be treated as freely usable merely because they are publicly available online.

---

## 11. APIs and External Services

B2C Solution may integrate third-party APIs and services.

These services may impose:

- Usage limits;
- Pricing;
- Authentication requirements;
- Data-processing requirements;
- Attribution requirements;
- Acceptable-use restrictions;
- Geographic restrictions;
- Service-specific terms.

The relevant third-party terms should be reviewed before integration.

---

## 12. Proprietary Third-Party Software

Some components may be commercially licensed rather than open-source.

Examples may include:

- Paid plugins;
- Commercial APIs;
- SaaS products;
- Design tools;
- Hosting services;
- Development platforms.

Commercial licenses should be obtained and maintained where required.

---

## 13. License Records

For significant projects, B2C Solution may maintain a project-specific third-party license record.

A recommended record format is:

| Component | Version | License | Source | Project | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Example Package | 1.0.0 | MIT | Official repository | Example Project | Attribution required |

The actual dependency list should be maintained separately for each project.

---

## 14. License Changes

Third-party licenses may change between releases.

Before upgrading a significant dependency, B2C Solution may review:

- New license terms;
- Copyright notices;
- Additional restrictions;
- Security implications;
- Compatibility requirements.

A new version should not automatically be assumed to have identical licensing terms.

---

## 15. Prohibited Practices

B2C Solution should not knowingly:

- Remove legally required copyright notices;
- Misrepresent third-party work as original B2C Solution work;
- Ignore applicable license requirements;
- Redistribute restricted software without authorization;
- Use assets outside their permitted license scope;
- Circumvent technical or contractual licensing restrictions.

---

## 16. Client Responsibility

Where a Client independently supplies or requests third-party software, assets, APIs, fonts, media, or other resources, the Client may be responsible for obtaining appropriate rights and licenses.

B2C Solution may request evidence of authorization where reasonably necessary.

---

## 17. License Compliance Review

For larger or higher-risk projects, B2C Solution may conduct a license review before final delivery.

The review may include:

- Dependency inventory;
- License identification;
- Attribution requirements;
- Copyright notices;
- Commercial-license verification;
- Security considerations.

---

## 18. Documentation

Where appropriate, project repositories may contain:

```text
LICENSE
NOTICE
THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md
package.json
package-lock.json
pnpm-lock.yaml
requirements.txt
```

Only files relevant to the particular technology stack need to be included.

---

## 19. Reporting a License Concern

If you believe that a B2C Solution project may be using third-party material in violation of its license, please contact us.

Include:

- Component or asset name;
- Project or website;
- Relevant license;
- Description of the concern;
- Supporting information;
- Recommended correction, if known.
